# Worked synthetic example

## Scenario

A client's business operating account is hacked in 2025. Unauthorized transfers total $45,000. The bank reimburses $5,000 and denies the rest. The client filed a police report. The packet does not establish whether the conduct is theft under the applicable state law.

## Result

The provisional net loss is $40,000. The analysis keeps business theft treatment and Form 4684 Section B in `review`. It requests a state-law classification memo or CPA and counsel conclusion before the treatment can be supported.

This is a successful result. The skill must preserve an unresolved legal element instead of converting a plausible fact pattern into a deduction.
