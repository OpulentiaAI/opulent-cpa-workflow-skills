# Loss treatment workflow

## Fact groups

Capture:

- property or funds and adjusted basis
- business, income-producing, employee, or personal use
- event and discovery dates
- how the event occurred
- police, bank, platform, insurer, or court records
- state-law classification
- reimbursements received
- claims, litigation, and reasonable prospects of recovery
- profit motive for the transaction
- related gains or prior deductions

## Decision order

1. Establish what property or funds were lost.
2. Establish whether the event meets the relevant legal classification.
3. Establish business, income-producing, or personal character.
4. Establish the measurement rule and adjusted basis.
5. Reduce for reimbursements and reasonable recovery prospects.
6. Establish the deduction year.
7. Apply federal limits and interactions.
8. Map the supported treatment to the current form.

## Treatment matrix

Include every plausible route raised by the facts, e.g. business theft, profit-motive scam theft, personal casualty or theft, loss on deposits, bad debt, capital loss, or no current deduction. A candidate remains `review` until all required elements and the tax-year authority are supported.

## Evidence boundary

State-law theft, reasonable prospect of recovery, profit motive, basis, fair market value, and business use are evidence questions. A fluent narrative is not proof. Ask for the document or fact that resolves each element.
