# Source map

Accessed August 24, 2026. Refresh official tax sources for the requested tax year.

## Tax authority

- [2025 Form 4684 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i4684). Use for business and personal casualty or theft sections and the IRS scam guidance summary.
- [IRS Publication 547 for 2025](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p547). Use for casualty, theft, proof, measurement, recovery, timing, and reporting paths.
- [IRS Topic 515](https://www.irs.gov/taxtopics/tc515). Use as a concise index to current casualty, disaster, and theft treatment.
