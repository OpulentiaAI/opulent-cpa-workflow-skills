# Multistate income workpaper

The fixture treats the taxpayer as a full-year Illinois resident. It allocates the mixed-state consulting item by documented service days, which produces $90,000 of Illinois source income and $30,000 of Arizona source income. Total state-source income is $840,000 for Illinois and $30,000 for Arizona.

The Illinois resident-return column includes all $870,000. The Arizona amount remains $30,000. CPA review must confirm the service-day method, current filing thresholds, Illinois resident instructions, Arizona nonresident instructions, and any Illinois credit for tax paid to Arizona.
