# Source map

Accessed August 24, 2026. Refresh state sources for the requested tax year.

## State authority for the fixture

- [2025 Illinois Schedule NR instructions](https://tax.illinois.gov/forms/incometax/currentyear/individual/il-1040-schedule-nr-instr.html). Use for Illinois nonresident and part-year source and apportionment rules. Confirm the separate full-year resident instructions when that branch applies.
- [2025 Arizona Form 140NR](https://azdor.gov/forms/individual/nonresident-personal-income-tax-form-calculating). Use for Arizona-source income and the current nonresident form and instructions.
- [Multistate Tax Commission adopted recommendations](https://www.mtc.gov/Uniformity/Adopted-Uniformity-Recommendations/). Use for model nexus and apportionment concepts. Verify what each state enacted.

## Construction and examples

- Randy Keller and Amir Curry, `opulent-cpa-workflow-explainer.md`. Workflow D supplies the multistate income, filing, allocation, and reasoning contract.
- [Letta creating-skills](https://github.com/letta-ai/letta-code/tree/main/src/skills/builtin/creating-skills) and [Matt Pocock writing for agents](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/writing-for-agents). Use for a concise workflow, evidence fields, and checkable completion.
- [APEX Accounting](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mercor/apex-accounting), [Harbor evals](https://www.harborframework.com/docs/run-jobs/run-evals), [OSWorld](https://osworld-v1.xlang.ai/), and the [Opulent demo suite](https://github.com/OpulentiaAI/knowledge-work-demo-suite). Use for synthetic worlds, task files, hidden criteria, computer-use trajectories, and validators.
- [Shortcut accounting cases](https://shortcut.ai/cases/how-accounting-teams-use-shortcut) show review flags and source-driven analysis. Treat vendor claims as examples.
- [Financial Datasets](https://www.financialdatasets.ai/) and the [CWU economic and financial datasets guide](https://libguides.lib.cwu.edu/c.php?g=358343&p=2419792) can supply public market or economic fixture data. They do not establish state tax law.
