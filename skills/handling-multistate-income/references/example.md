# Worked synthetic example

## Scenario

The taxpayer is a full-year Illinois resident. The 2025 source packet has $450,000 of Illinois W-2 wages, $120,000 of 1099 consulting income performed over 150 Illinois days and 50 Arizona days, and $300,000 of Illinois consulting income.

## Allocation fixture

The sample assumes a CPA-approved service-day method for the first 1099 item. It allocates $90,000 to Illinois and $30,000 to Arizona. Total source income is $840,000 for Illinois and $30,000 for Arizona. The Illinois resident-return column includes the full $870,000, while the Arizona nonresident column includes $30,000.

The output marks an Illinois credit for tax paid to Arizona as a review item. A production run must verify sourcing, filing thresholds, return forms, and credit rules from current state authority.
