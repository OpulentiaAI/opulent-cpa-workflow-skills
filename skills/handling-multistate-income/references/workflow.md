# Multistate workflow

## Fact matrix

Capture these facts by date and state:

- domicile and statutory residency
- homes and lease periods
- physical workdays and service locations
- employer and payer records
- W-2 state wages and withholding
- 1099 income and where services were performed
- entity property, payroll, sales, contractors, and registrations
- pass-through schedules and composite filings
- prior returns, notices, and credits

## Separate questions

Answer each question independently:

1. Is the taxpayer a resident, part-year resident, or nonresident?
2. What income is sourced to the state?
3. Does the taxpayer or entity have filing or registration nexus?
4. Does a threshold or exception apply for the stated tax year?
5. Which allocation or apportionment formula applies to this income type?
6. Is withholding, a composite return, reciprocity, or a credit relevant?

## Allocation record

For each income item, store the gross amount, states, numerator, denominator, allocation method, source facts, amount by state, rule URL, and status. Allocated amounts must sum to gross income unless the workpaper explains a valid overlap or exclusion.

## Filing determination

Use `required` or `not_required` only when facts and current state authority support the answer. Use `review` when residency, source, threshold, legal entity, treaty, reciprocity, or tax-year authority is incomplete.

## Source precedence

Use the official revenue department, current form instructions, statutes, and regulations for the state and tax year. Use federal forms as income evidence. Use MTC material for model concepts only. Use vendor or benchmark material for task design only.
