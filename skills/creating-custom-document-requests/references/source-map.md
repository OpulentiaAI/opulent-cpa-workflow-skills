# Source map

Accessed August 24, 2026. Refresh form and record requirements for the requested tax year.

- Randy Keller and Amir Curry, `opulent-cpa-workflow-explainer.md`. Workflow F defines the professional baseline plus transcript-driven custom addendum.
- [IRS Publication 583](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p583). Use for business record and supporting-document categories.
- [2025 Schedule C instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sc), [Publication 463](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p463), [Publication 946](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946), and [Form 4684 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i4684). Use to identify tax-year evidence needs for business activity, vehicles, assets, and loss events.
- [Letta creating-skills](https://github.com/letta-ai/letta-code/tree/main/src/skills/builtin/creating-skills) and [Matt Pocock writing for agents](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/writing-for-agents). Use for the two-stage workflow, source traceability, and deterministic completion checks.
- [APEX Accounting](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mercor/apex-accounting), [Harbor evals](https://www.harborframework.com/docs/run-jobs/run-evals), [OSWorld](https://osworld-v1.xlang.ai/), and the [Opulent demo suite](https://github.com/OpulentiaAI/knowledge-work-demo-suite). Use for file-rich worlds, hidden cue lists, test scripts, computer-use trajectories, and source provenance.
- [Shortcut cases](https://shortcut.ai/cases), [Financial Datasets](https://www.financialdatasets.ai/), and the [CWU data guide](https://libguides.lib.cwu.edu/c.php?g=358343&p=2419792) can supply public examples or fixture data. They do not define a client's required documents.
