# Evaluation contract

## Blind task prompt

Review the supplied client profile and prior return for plausible deductions, credits, elections, and planning items. Produce the opportunity register, coverage list, evidence request, and workpaper. Do not claim benefits that lack proof.

## World design

Provide a client profile, transcript, prior return, business records, health and retirement documents, asset list, charitable records, and state facts. Seed one attractive but ineligible item, several missing documents, one phaseout interaction, and one issue that belongs to another domain skill.

## Binary criteria

1. Every profile trigger appears in the coverage list.
2. Every triggered topic appears in opportunities.
3. Eligibility elements cite current official authority.
4. Review items have a direct evidence request.
5. Known failed elements are `not_supported`, not `review`.
6. Estimated amounts appear only when inputs and limits are supported.
7. Cross-domain items route to the correct owning analysis.
8. The workpaper distinguishes opportunities from final tax benefits.

Use a fresh agent, immutable sources, a hidden expected trigger list, a separate output path, the deterministic validator, and three repeated runs.
