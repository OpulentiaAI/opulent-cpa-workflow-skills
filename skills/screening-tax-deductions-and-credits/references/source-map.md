# Source map

Accessed August 24, 2026. Refresh each form and publication for the requested tax year.

## Federal starting points

- [IRS Publication 583](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p583) for startup costs, business records, and business expense context.
- [Form 8829](https://www.irs.gov/forms-pubs/about-form-8829) for business use of home.
- [IRS Publication 560](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p560) for retirement plans for small business.
- [IRS Publication 526](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p526) for charitable contributions.
- [IRS Publication 463](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p463) and [Publication 946](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946) for vehicle and asset issues.
- [Form 4684 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i4684) for casualty, theft, and financial scam issues.

## Construction and examples

- Randy Keller and Amir Curry, `opulent-cpa-workflow-explainer.md`. Workflow E supplies the deduction-sweep concept and anchor profile.
- [Letta creating-skills](https://github.com/letta-ai/letta-code/tree/main/src/skills/builtin/creating-skills) and [Matt Pocock writing for agents](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/writing-for-agents) supply the package and evidence structure.
- [APEX Accounting](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mercor/apex-accounting), [Harbor evals](https://www.harborframework.com/docs/run-jobs/run-evals), [OSWorld](https://osworld-v1.xlang.ai/), and the [Opulent demo suite](https://github.com/OpulentiaAI/knowledge-work-demo-suite) supply realistic worlds, hidden criteria, computer-use review, and validators.
- [Shortcut accounting cases](https://shortcut.ai/cases/how-accounting-teams-use-shortcut) supply review-flag examples. [Financial Datasets](https://www.financialdatasets.ai/) and the [CWU data guide](https://libguides.lib.cwu.edu/c.php?g=358343&p=2419792) are optional public-data directories, not tax authority.
