# Source map

Accessed August 24, 2026. Use the authority from the approved domain workpaper and refresh it for the requested tax year.

- Randy Keller and Amir Curry, `opulent-cpa-workflow-explainer.md`. The cross-cutting memo section supplies the rule, numbers, result, and planning-note pattern.
- [IRS Publication 946 for 2025](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946), [Publication 463](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p463), and [Form 4562 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i4562) support the bundled vehicle example.
- [Letta creating-skills](https://github.com/letta-ai/letta-code/tree/main/src/skills/builtin/creating-skills) and [Matt Pocock writing for agents](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/writing-for-agents) support progressive disclosure, output examples, evidence fields, and completion checks.
- [APEX Accounting](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mercor/apex-accounting) supports one-outcome-per-criterion fact checks and golden response patterns.
- [Harbor evals](https://www.harborframework.com/docs/run-jobs/run-evals), [OSWorld](https://osworld-v1.xlang.ai/), and the [Opulent demo suite](https://github.com/OpulentiaAI/knowledge-work-demo-suite) support isolated tasks, trajectory review, immutable sources, and output validation.
- [Shortcut accounting cases](https://shortcut.ai/cases/how-accounting-teams-use-shortcut) show plain review flags and management commentary. Treat vendor claims as examples.
- [Financial Datasets](https://www.financialdatasets.ai/) and the [CWU data guide](https://libguides.lib.cwu.edu/c.php?g=358343&p=2419792) can support a public-data memo when relevant. They are not substitutes for tax authority.
