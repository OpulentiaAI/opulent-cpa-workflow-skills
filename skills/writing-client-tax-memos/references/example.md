# Worked synthetic example

## Scenario

The client expected a full vehicle write-off. The approved sample workpaper shows $65,000 of basis, 6,000 business miles, 20,000 total miles, 30 percent qualified business use, and a more-than-50-percent test for the Section 179 and special allowance routes. The fixture deduction is $1,950 under the method supplied in the sample.

## Memo choice

Lead with the result. Explain the 30 percent calculation next. State that the workpaper does not support the expected full write-off. Keep the future-use note conditional and mention recordkeeping and recapture review.

The sample memo includes source links because the user asked for accurate references. A CPA may remove those links from the client version after review.
