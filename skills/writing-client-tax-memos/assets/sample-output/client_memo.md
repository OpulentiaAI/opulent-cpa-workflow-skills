# Subject: 2025 vehicle deduction review

## What we found

Your records support 30 percent business use for the vehicle. The current workpaper does not support the full write-off you expected. The sample schedule shows a 2025 deduction of $1,950 under the method approved for this fixture.

## How we calculated it

The vehicle tax basis is $65,000. Your log shows 6,000 business miles and 20,000 total miles. Dividing 6,000 by 20,000 gives 30 percent business use.

The listed-property rule requires more than 50 percent qualified business use for the Section 179 and special depreciation allowance routes reviewed in the workpaper. At 30 percent, those routes are not supported by the current facts.

For comparison, the same sample method at 100 percent business use would produce $6,500. That comparison changes only the business-use percentage. It is not a promise of a future deduction.

## What remains open

Your CPA must replace the synthetic fixture method with the approved filing method and current limits before this amount can be used on a return.

## What to do next

Keep a complete mileage log and send any missing trips to your CPA. If your business use changes, your CPA can review the options and any recapture effect under the rules for that year.
