# Vehicle depreciation workpaper

Qualified business use is 6,000 miles divided by 20,000 total miles, or 30 percent. This does not meet the more-than-50-percent predominant business use test described in 2025 Publication 946.

Included basis is $65,000. Business basis is $19,500. The sample applies a synthetic 10 percent rate supplied only for arithmetic testing, which gives $1,950. The same fixture rate at 100 percent business use gives $6,500.

CPA review must resolve the service contract, confirm ADS recovery period and convention, apply passenger-auto limits, verify current Form 4562 placement, and replace the fixture rate with the approved current method.
