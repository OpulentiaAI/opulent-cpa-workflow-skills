# Source map

Accessed August 24, 2026. Refresh official tax sources for the requested tax year.

## Tax authority

- [IRS Publication 946 for 2025](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p946). Use for basis, listed property, predominant business use, methods, recovery periods, and recapture.
- [IRS Publication 463 for 2025](https://www.irs.gov/publications/p463). Use for vehicle use, mileage, substantiation, and Section 179 context.
- [2025 Form 4562 instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i4562). Use for depreciation and listed-property form mapping.
- [2025 Schedule C instructions](https://www.irs.gov/instructions/i1040sc). Use for Schedule C vehicle expense and depreciation placement.

## Construction and examples

- Randy Keller and Amir Curry, `opulent-cpa-workflow-explainer.md`. Workflow C supplies the extract, test, calculate, counterfactual, map, and explain loop.
- [Letta creating-skills](https://github.com/letta-ai/letta-code/tree/main/src/skills/builtin/creating-skills) and [Matt Pocock writing for agents](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills/tree/main/skills/productivity/writing-for-agents). Use for progressive disclosure, evidence fields, and checkable completion.
- [APEX Accounting](https://huggingface.co/datasets/mercor/apex-accounting), [Harbor evals](https://www.harborframework.com/docs/run-jobs/run-evals), [OSWorld](https://osworld-v1.xlang.ai/), and the [Opulent demo suite](https://github.com/OpulentiaAI/knowledge-work-demo-suite). Use for realistic worlds, hidden graders, deterministic checks, visible-computer trajectories, and provenance.
- [Shortcut cases](https://shortcut.ai/cases) provides product examples of document-to-workbook automation and review flags.
- [Financial Datasets](https://www.financialdatasets.ai/) and the [CWU data guide](https://libguides.lib.cwu.edu/c.php?g=358343&p=2419792) are optional public-data sources. They are not depreciation authority.
