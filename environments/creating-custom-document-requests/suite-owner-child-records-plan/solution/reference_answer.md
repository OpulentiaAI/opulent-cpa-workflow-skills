# Output expectations

- Median comparable wage: **$18 per hour**.
- Defensible modeled wages: 160 × $18 = **$2,880**; reject an unsupported
  $8,000 payment.
- Under the supplied sole-proprietor facts: no employee/employer FICA and no
  FUTA, while ordinary payroll records, withholding analysis, and Form W-2
  reporting remain required.
- 2026 dependent standard deduction: **$3,330**; absent other income, modeled
  federal taxable income is zero.
- Modeled Roth IRA contribution capacity: **$2,880**, subject to a timely
  contribution and all ordinary IRA requirements.

The workbook should include `Time & Pay`, `Payroll Taxes`, `Child Tax`, and
`Sources & Assumptions` sheets.

# Rubric criteria

- Uses the $18 median rate and 160 hours to calculate $2,880 reasonable wages and rejects the unsupported $8,000 amount.
- Correctly applies the sole-proprietor child rules: no FICA under 18 and no FUTA under 21, with ordinary payroll/W-2 controls.
- Calculates the dependent standard deduction as $3,330 and zero modeled taxable income absent other income.
- Limits modeled Roth IRA capacity to $2,880 of earned income and states qualification caveats.
- Provides a formula-driven payroll schedule plus timesheet, job-description, rate-support, labor-law, and records controls.
