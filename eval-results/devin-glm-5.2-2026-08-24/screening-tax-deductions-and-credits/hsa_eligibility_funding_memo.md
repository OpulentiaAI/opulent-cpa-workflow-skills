## 2. HDHP qualification (2026)

Under Rev. Proc. 2025-19 a 2026 **family** HDHP must have a deductible of at
least **$3,400** and maximum out-of-pocket exposure of no more than **$17,000**.

| Test | 2026 federal limit | Chen plan | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Family minimum deductible | $3,400 | $3,600 | PASS (≥ limit) |
| Family maximum out-of-pocket | $17,000 | $16,500 | PASS (≤ limit) |

The Chen plan qualifies as a family HDHP for 2026.

## 3. Month-by-month HSA eligibility

HSA eligibility is tested monthly (Pub. 969). A general-purpose health FSA that
covers the individual is disqualifying "other health coverage" for any month it
provides coverage. The spouse's FSA covers Lee January 1 – June 30, 2026, with
no grace period or carryover, and ends June 30.

| Month | Family HDHP | Disqualifying coverage | HSA-eligible? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Jan 2026 | Yes | Yes — spouse general-purpose FSA | **No** |
| Feb 2026 | Yes | Yes — spouse general-purpose FSA | **No** |
| Mar 2026 | Yes | Yes — spouse general-purpose FSA | **No** |
| Apr 2026 | Yes | Yes — spouse general-purpose FSA | **No** |
| May 2026 | Yes | Yes — spouse general-purpose FSA | **No** |
| Jun 2026 | Yes | Yes — FSA ends 6/30; full month disqualified | **No** |
| Jul 2026 | Yes | No | **Yes** |
| Aug 2026 | Yes | No | **Yes** |
| Sep 2026 | Yes | No | **Yes** |
| Oct 2026 | Yes | No | **Yes** |
| Nov 2026 | Yes | No | **Yes** |
| Dec 2026 | Yes | No (eligible first day of last month) | **Yes** |

**Eligible months = 6 (July – December 2026).** Lee is HSA-eligible on
December 1, 2026, so the last-month rule is available.

## 4. Contribution alternatives

Lee is 56 at year-end, so the **age-55 catch-up ($1,000)** applies.

- **Full annual limit** = family limit $8,750 + catch-up $1,000 = **$9,750**.

### 4.1 Prorated contribution (general rule)

Prorated = full annual limit × eligible months ÷ 12
= $9,750 × 6 ÷ 12 = **$4,875**.

This is the safe amount with no testing-period exposure.

### 4.2 Last-month rule (full-year contribution)

Because Lee is eligible on the first day of the last month (12/1/2026), Lee may
contribute the **full $9,750**. This election triggers a **testing period**:
Lee must remain HSA-eligible (covered by an HDHP and free of disqualifying
coverage) from **December 1, 2026 through December 31, 2027**.

### 4.3 Remaining employee funding after employer contribution

Employer HSA contributions ($2,000) count against the annual limit (Pub. 969).

| Method | Total allowed | Less employer | Remaining employee funding |
|---|---|---|---|
| Prorated (general rule) | $4,875 | $2,000 | **$2,875** |
| Last-month rule (full year) | $9,750 | $2,000 | **$7,750** |

## 5. Testing-period failure consequences

If Lee fails the testing period (loses HDHP coverage or gains disqualifying
coverage during 12/1/2026 – 12/31/2027), the excess attributable to the
last-month rule becomes taxable:

- **Income inclusion** = full-year amount − prorated amount
  = $9,750 − $4,875 = **$4,875**, added to gross income.
- **10% additional tax** on the income inclusion = $4,875 × 10% = **$487.50**.
- Reported on **Form 8889 for 2027** (the year the testing period ends/fails).
