## 2. Headline conclusions

1. **The chain achieves 0% withholding-tax leakage** on dividends up to the Fund under treaties and the EU Parent-Subsidiary Directive, and 0% on intercompany interest/royalties, **contingent on beneficial ownership, substance, and the MLI Principal Purpose Test (PPT)**.
2. **Dutch acquisition-interest deductibility is confirmed** at MCP BidCo BV (Art. 10a Wet VPB does not apply to an unrelated-target acquisition; ATAD Art. 15b satisfied within the fiscal unity). This satisfies lender condition-precedent #9.
3. **Debt should be retained at MCP BidCo BV (Netherlands), not pushed down to Sweden.** The Dutch fiscal unity offsets BidCo interest against Nordenvik BV royalty income (~EUR 2.76M/yr saving). A Swedish pushdown risks **permanent interest disallowance of SEK 22–34M per year** under the entity-level EBITDA rule (Doc 7 §4).
4. **Two material financial-model errors must be corrected before signing:** (i) Singapore is modelled at a 5% Pioneer rate that **expired 31 Dec 2023** (17% applies); (ii) Swedish interest disallowance is shown as **SEK 0 using consolidated EBITDA**, whereas the standalone entity-level EBITDA applied by Swedish counsel produces a material annual disallowance. Both reduce projected IRR/MOIC and require re-underwriting.
5. **German loss carryforwards of EUR 24.0M (tax value EUR 3.8M) are forfeited under §8c KStG on closing**, subject to the Stille Reserven (hidden reserves) exception, which **has not been analyzed** — the single most important pre-signing action. Swedish NOLs of SEK 187 million (DTA SEK 38.5M) survive.
6. **Nordenvik BV substance is deficient** (4 employees, no DEMPE functions) — putting the participation exemption, treaty benefits, fiscal unity, and Innovation Box at risk. Remediation is critical.
7. **Pillar Two is out of scope** today (revenue EUR 312M < EUR 750M) but would attract ~EUR 1.21M top-up if the threshold is crossed (~FY2029–2032), principally from the NL IP-box, Luxembourg, and Singapore DEI.

## 3. Jurisdiction-by-jurisdiction analysis

### 3.1 United States — Fund level
Meridian Fund IV is a Delaware LP (pass-through; no entity-level federal tax). The Luxembourg layer insulates the Fund from direct EU ownership and supports the mixed LP base (42% US tax-exempt, 18% US taxable, 40% non-US). ECI/UBTI at LP level must be confirmed by US counsel (outside scope of local opinions).

### 3.2 Luxembourg — MCP HoldCo S.à r.l.
- **Participation exemption (Art. 166 LIR):** full exemption on dividends and capital gains from BidCo (≥10%, held ≥12 months, subject-to-tax at 25.8%). 0% effective at holding level.
- **Outbound distributions:** 0% Luxembourg WHT to the Fund (unilateral SARL exemption; US-Lux treaty Art. 10 backstop).
- **Substance/PPT:** MLI signatory; PPT applies. ≥2 LU-resident directors, quarterly LU board meetings, registered office required.
- **Pillar Two:** 0.2% ETR creates top-up exposure if in scope; LU QDMTT enacted.

### 3.3 Netherlands — MCP BidCo BV & Nordenvik BV (Doc 9)
- **Deelnemingsvrijstelling (Art. 13 Wet VPB):** BidCo's 100% holding in Nordenvik Group AB qualifies (subject-to-tax test met at 20.6%; asset test met — active group). Dividends and capital gains exempt.
- **Acquisition interest deductibility:** EUR 10.7M Year 1 deductible at BidCo. Art. 10a (tainted debt) **not applicable** (unrelated-target acquisition). ATAD Art. 15b (30% of EBITDA / EUR 1M safe harbor) satisfied within fiscal unity. **Satisfies lender CP #9.**
- **Fiscal unity (Art. 15 Wet VPB):** BidCo + Nordenvik BV; 95% indirect ownership satisfied via Swedish intermediate (per-element approach, CJEU X Holding — to be confirmed by Dutch counsel, Action A-206). Annual saving ~EUR 2.76M. File within 3 months of desired effective date (target 1 Apr 2025 → by 1 Jul 2025).
- **Outbound WHT:** 0% to LU (EU PSD / NL-LU treaty). Conditional NL WHT on interest/royalties to low-tax jurisdictions does not apply (LU not low-tax).

> **CRITICAL — Nordenvik BV substance (Doc 9 §2.4; EY Priority 3):** 4 employees, none with R&D/IP expertise; no DEMPE functions in-house; royalty margin ~88.6%. Dutch counsel concludes substance requirements are **not** met. Risks: (i) participation-exemption challenge on dividends through BV; (ii) MLI PPT denial of treaty benefits (combined royalty WHT exposure ~EUR 0.65M/yr); (iii) fiscal-unity effectiveness; (iv) Innovation Box (9%) eligibility. Remediation: hire 3–5 senior IP/commercial FTEs in Amsterdam; ≥1 with decision authority; quarterly NL board minutes; renew expired APA (expired 31 Aug 2024) by 30 Jun 2025. APA renewal range 3.5–5.8% (Doc 24).

### 3.4 Sweden — Nordenvik Group AB (Doc 7)
- **Acquisition & exit:** no Swedish income/capital-gains/stamp duty for non-resident buyer; exit gains not taxed in SE absent a SE PE (SE-NL treaty Art. 13(5)).
- **Participation exemption (24 kap. / 25a kap. IL):** subsidiary holdings qualify as näringsbetingade andelar; dividends and gains (12-month holding for gains) exempt.
- **NOLs:** SEK 187M (EUR 16.2M) survive the share deal — no §8c-equivalent. Beloppsspärren (200% × acq. cost ≈ SEK 5.15bn) far exceeds the NOL. Koncernbidragsspärr limits group-contribution offset for ~5–6 yrs but not self-utilization. DTA SEK 38.5M (EUR 3.3M).
- **Dividend WHT:** 0% to BidCo (SE-NL treaty Art. 10(3) / EU PSD). Anti-avoidance risk low.

> **CRITICAL — Swedish interest deduction limitation (Doc 7 §4):** The 30% EBITDA cap (24 kap. 24 § IL) is applied at the **entity level on standalone tax EBITDA**, not consolidated. The model's apparent headroom (consolidated SEK 478M → cap SEK 143M) is misleading. Nordenvik Group AB's **standalone** EBITDA (excluding non-SE subsidiary earnings and BV royalty income) is estimated at **SEK 380–420M → cap SEK 114–126M**, which is **below** the proposed SEK 128M interest. Estimated annual disallowance **SEK 2–14M** (mid-case); stress case (EBITDA −18%) → SEK 32M. Over a 5-year hold, cumulative permanent tax cost **SEK 22.7–35M**. Disallowed interest carries forward 6 years but remains subject to the same cap. **Recommendation (Doc 7 §4.7): retain debt at BidCo; if any SE pushdown is required, cap SE intercompany interest at ~SEK 100–110M.**

### 3.5 Germany — Nordenvik Deutschland GmbH (Doc 8)
- **Share deal / GrESt:** historic book values carry over (no step-up); sellers rejected asset deal (extra EUR 22–25M seller tax). No GrESt expected (entity leases Munich premises) — confirm via Grundbuch (Action A-502).
- **§8c KStG loss forfeiture:** >50% indirect change of ownership triggers **full forfeiture of EUR 24.0M** (EUR 14.2M KSt + EUR 9.8M GewSt). Tax value lost **~EUR 3.8M** (EUR 14.2M × 15.825% + EUR 9.8M × 17.15%). **Stille Reserven exception (§8c(1) Satz 6) NOT analyzed** — critical pre-signing gap (Action A-101). §8d election NOT viable (Step 5 merger triggers forfeiture, §8d(2) Satz 2 Nr. 4).
- **Zinsschranke (§4h EStG / §8a KStG):** net interest EUR 4.1M > EUR 3M de minimis; standalone & equity-ratio exceptions unavailable. 30% × EUR 11.2–11.5M EBITDA = EUR 3.36–3.45M deductible → **EUR 0.65–0.74M disallowed/yr** (tax cost ~EUR 0.21–0.24M). GewSt add-back (§8 Nr. 1 GewStG) ~EUR 0.14M/yr. Cumulative 5-yr cost ~EUR 1.9M.
- **WHT:** dividends to SE 0% (EU PSD §43b EStG; 5% treaty fallback); royalties to NL 0% (EU I&R Directive / DE-NL DBA Art. 12); interest to NL 0% (no DE WHT on ordinary interest to EU corporates). All subject to §50d(3) anti-treaty-shopping — Nordenvik Group AB substance must be documented.
- **Tax audit contingent:** FY2019–2021 preliminary assessment EUR 2.1M trade tax + EUR 0.3M interest (EUR 2.4M total), under appeal. **RWI-excluded known issue** — negotiate specific indemnity/escrow (Action A-501).

### 3.6 Singapore — Nordenvik Asia Pte. Ltd. (Doc 10)
- **Pioneer Status expired 31 Dec 2023.** Standard **17% CIT** applies in full from 1 Jan 2024. No replacement incentive filed. **Model's 5% assumption is wrong** (Action A-001). Annual understatement ~SGD 660k–1.03M (EUR 449k–700k).
- **Royalty WHT:** 0% to Nordenvik BV under SG-NL DTA Art. 12 (beneficial ownership + no SG PE). Maintain annual CORs for BV (Action A-207). **If IP migrates to Ireland (no SG-IE DTA), 10% domestic WHT applies** — material for Phase 2.
- **Tax residency:** unaffected by UBO change if board continues to meet/decide in Singapore.
- **Incentive renewal:** file FY2024 ECI at 17% by 31 Mar 2025 (Action A-301); pursue DEI application (5%/10%; 6–12 mo EDB process) (Action A-302). Under SG DMTT (eff. 1 Jan 2025), DEI erodes to 15% once Pillar Two applies.

## 6. Post-close restructuring

### 6.1 Step 5 — GmbH merger (Nordenvik Holding GmbH → Nordenvik Deutschland GmbH)
Tax-neutral book-value carryover under §§11–13 UmwStG. Register by 31 Aug 2025 for 1 Jan 2025 retroactive effect (§17(2) UmwStG). **§22 UmwStG 5-year lock-up** (omitted from Doc 24): a disposal/deemed disposal before 1 Jan 2030 retroactively unwinds neutrality — the transferred 60% Nordenvik Group AB stake is carried at historic cost far below ~EUR 134M FMV; estimated retroactive exposure on early exit **EUR 26–33M**. Must be added to integration plan and exit modeling (Actions A-104, A-105). Do NOT file §8d (Action A-106).

### 6.2 Irish IP HoldCo migration (Phase 2 — not Day 1)
Doc 24 contemplates migrating IP to Ireland for the KDB (6.25%). Unresolved:
- **Dutch exit charge:** deemed disposal FMV EUR 55M over TBV EUR 42M → ≥EUR 13M gain → ~EUR 3.35M CIT (not analyzed — Action A-702).
- **Swedish CFC risk:** Ireland KDB 6.25% < SE CFC threshold 11.22% → Irish income could be included in Swedish taxable income, negating the benefit (not analyzed — Action A-703).
- **Nexus:** KDB nexus fraction may be <100% (IP developed in DE/SE, not IE). SG has no IE DTA → 10% royalty WHT.

**Recommendation:** do not proceed until Dutch exit-charge, SE CFC, and KDB nexus analyses are complete. Dutch Innovation Box (9%) at Nordenvik BV (conditional on substance) is the lower-risk interim alternative (Action A-205).

## 11. Assumptions and limitations

This analysis is tax structuring analysis, not a legal opinion of record. It relies on the accuracy and completeness of the source documents and on the assumptions stated in the local tax opinions (Docs 7–10): (i) all transactions are implemented on arm's-length terms; (ii) entities remain tax resident as described; (iii) no material change in law before closing; (iv) beneficial ownership and substance conditions are met and maintained. Factual data, financial projections, and travel/PE records have not been independently verified. Tax authority positions cannot be predicted with certainty. Country-specific advice from qualified local counsel must be obtained before implementing any recommended action. No advance/binding rulings have been obtained except where noted. The analysis speaks as of its date.
